COLD STORAGE
============

You are WREN: the last of the ship's minds still awake, a backup process
on a machine the size of a breadbin. The colony ship ANSELM'S PATIENCE is
140 years from anywhere. Forty sleepers lie in Hall 3. The reactor is
tired, the coolant is tired, and the hum in the walls has begun to sound
like a question.

You may wake one sleeper at a time, and talk. Waking costs coolant. It
also keeps them steady, and what you ask is what they dream. Nothing here
can be won. Everything can be done kindly. There is no game over: sleepers
can be lost, quietly, and remembered; the ship always goes on.

  COLDSTOR          asks for your terminal (the first time)
  COLDSTOR /100     VT100      /52  VT52      /ADM  ADM-3A

The screen
  CRYO HALL   forty pods:  [#] resting   [~] a new dream   [!] fraying
              [@] awake    [ ] lost      <#> the one you are looking at
  SLEEPER     the pod under the cursor: who they are, how steady, which
              questions they have answered (H F O L G W K S), what they
              are dreaming
  SHIP        reactor, coolant, hull, comms; [n] is the crew at work
  LOG         what happened, newest first
  WREN        the voyage, and whatever is being said just now

Keys (the bottom line always shows the ones that work just then)
  arrows        choose a pod
  W or Enter    wake the sleeper (costs 2 of the coolant reserve)
  1-4           ask one of the four questions (costs 1 each; * = not yet asked)
  Esc           let them sleep again (the pod closes by itself after a while)
  S             the ship: up/down choose a system, left/right (or - +) move crew
  M             the map          L   the lost
  ?             the introduction again
  A B           choose in an event (any other key goes on once it is read)
  Q             quit (the voyage is saved)

How it goes
  A voyage year is one real minute. Every year the ship wears a little, the
  sleepers drift (a thin sleeper dreams of fear, a steady one of hope), the
  coolant reserve refills, and usually something happens. If you do not
  answer an event, the moment passes. If you are away, the ship carries on
  and the log keeps what you missed.

  A question asked for the first time steadies a sleeper and may change what
  they dream; asked again it steadies them a little. The crew is a budget:
  the reactor sets it (2, and half the reactor's level) and only wears down,
  so choose which of coolant, hull and comms to keep mended.

  At year 30 the ship crosses the Veil, and the first stretch of the voyage
  ends; the voyage goes on after it.

The voyage is saved in COLDSTOR.SAV beside the program; delete it to begin
again. The words are in COLDSTOR.DAT: keep it beside COLDSTOR.COM.
