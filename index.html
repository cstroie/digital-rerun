DRIVES

ADD FILES (or drop files here) copies them to the selected drive; then press ^C at the prompt. Tap a file to download it.

SHELF

not connected

Your own catalog on your own web server: disks you may not share are fetched from there when you insert them, and are never part of this app. The address and token stay in this browser.

Disks you keep here, from a drive or from a file on this device.

My disks are kept in this browser only: your own software (WordStar, MBASIC …) stays on your devices. After inserting a disk, press ^C at the prompt.

PRINTER FX-80

CP/M prints to LST:: ^P echoes the console, PIP LST:=FILE.TXT prints a file.

PAPER TAPE

Reader: no tape. Punch: nothing yet. PIP FILE.TXT=RDR: reads the tape, PIP PUN:=FILE.TXT punches a file.

ONLINE

MODEM

INTERNAL dials the BBS: TERM, then ATDT5550100, or a telnet host through a bridge you run (node tools/modem-bridge.js --allow=host, its address and token above). EXTERNAL uses a real modem on a USB serial port instead (Chrome or Edge); set the speed to match.

SYSOP CHAT

The BBS sysop (C at its menu) answers from built-in rules. With Online services on and an endpoint here (Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server), a language model answers instead. It gets only the sysop's instructions, the recent chat and your line. The key stays in this browser. A local server must allow this page's origin (CORS).

SETUP

APP

Install your programs for the terminal chosen here (WordStar, Turbo Pascal and most others list both). ^C warm start, ^S pause, ^P printer echo, ^X cancel line.

SNAPSHOTS

A snapshot keeps the CPU, the memory, the screen and a copy of the disks. RESTORE puts the machine back to that moment; the printed paper stays as it is.
